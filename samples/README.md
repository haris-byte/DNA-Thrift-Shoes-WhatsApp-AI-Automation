# Sample images

`sample_size_tag.png` is only a deterministic OCR setup check. It is not evidence of real shoe recognition.

For the final demonstration, use:

1. A clear full/side shoe photo showing brand and shape.
2. A close size-tag photo where `US`, `UK`, or `EU` markings are visible.
3. A deliberately blurry or tag-hidden photo to demonstrate the clarification fallback.

The `/dev/upload` endpoint accepts a real JPEG, PNG, or WEBP upload directly from Swagger UI.
